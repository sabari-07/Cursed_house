# Requirements: Level 2 (The Library of Whispers) and Level 3 (The Crypt Beneath)

## Introduction

Levels 2 and 3 use the same rules and engine as Level 1
(`levels-and-leaderboard`):

- one room per level, with five stages played in it
- one plain-English question per stage, kept at the top of the screen
- one chance, and the ANSWER window
- a seeded answer, so it changes every run
- the witch death, and resuming the same stage after a death
- the level's treasure after Stage 5

Each room is a painting from the project owner:

- **The library:** `public/rooms/library.webp`, 1672 × 941.
- **The crypt:** `public/rooms/crypt.webp`, 1672 × 941.

Both levels are harder than Level 1. The rules come from `LEVELS` in
`src/game/levels.ts`:

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Time for the stage | 180 s | 150 s | 120 s |
| Time to answer | 15 s | 15 s | 12 s |
| Clues per answer | 2 or more | 3 or more | 3 or more |
| Red herrings per stage | 1 or more | 2 or more | 3 or more |

## Glossary

- **Room scene:** the shared renderer for a painted room. It handles the
  painting plate, Arjun, the clue overlays, inspecting objects, and the witch
  death.
- **Overlay:** something drawn onto the painting in the painting's perspective,
  such as a name plate's text, a book spine's colour, a reflection or a lamp's
  flame.
- **Choice answer:** the player picks one item from a small set of pictures.
- **Sequence answer:** the player puts four symbols in order.

## Requirement 1: A room scene for any painting

1. THE system SHALL render Levels 2 and 3 with one shared room scene. It SHALL
   be configured by data: the painting, the inspectable objects in painting px,
   the overlays, Arjun's place and the lights.
2. Arjun SHALL stand in each room, using the same 3D model, lit to match the
   room.
3. THE witch death SHALL be the same as in the hall (Arjun screams, blackout,
   the grip, shatter, blood, GAME OVER). It SHALL be shared code, not a copy.
4. THE room's shader SHALL compile off the main thread during the level intro.
   No frame during play SHALL take longer than 300 ms.
5. Light SHALL follow the house's rules: lamps and candles flicker as one value
   for the whole light, never as moving noise.

## Requirement 2: Entering Levels 2 and 3

1. WHEN the player enters the bungalow on Level 2 or 3 THEN a short intro SHALL
   lead into that room, with no new page:
   - **Library:** the hall's study door opens onto the library, and the voice
     whispers.
   - **Crypt:** stairs lead down, and the bell tolls once.
2. Each level SHALL show "LEVEL n · STAGE m" and the stage's name, as in Level 1.

## Requirement 3: Level 2 stages, in the library

| Stage | Name | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Red Books | a number |
| 2 | The Mirror | a five-letter word |
| 3 | The Whispering Book | a choice of four spine symbols |
| 4 | The Wandering Ladder | a number |
| 5 | The Locked Cabinet | a sequence of four symbols |

Each SHALL need at least 3 clues and plant at least 2 red herrings (design.md).

## Requirement 4: Level 3 stages, in the crypt

| Stage | Name | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Who Died Youngest | a choice of six name plates |
| 2 | The Bell | a number |
| 3 | Words in the Water | a five-letter word |
| 4 | The Order of the Dead | a sequence of four symbols |
| 5 | The Sealed Door | a number (a year) |

Each SHALL need at least 3 clues and plant at least 3 red herrings (design.md).

## Requirement 5: New answer kinds

1. A **choice** answer SHALL show its options as pictures with names. Picking
   one selects it.
2. A **sequence** answer SHALL show four slots and the four symbols (the eye,
   the raven, the hourglass, the key). Clicking a symbol fills the next slot;
   clicking a slot clears it.
3. Both SHALL use the same one-chance confirmation and answer window as the
   other kinds.

## Requirement 6: Fairness

1. For every seed, the clues in the scene SHALL allow exactly one answer. The
   tests SHALL prove it over 1,000 seeds per stage.
2. Every clue SHALL be readable, either from the wide view or when inspected.
   Nothing SHALL hide a clue for more than 2 seconds.
3. A clue that is only heard (the bell, the whisper) SHALL also be shown, as
   subtitles and as something visible. Sound is never the only way to find an
   answer.
4. Questions SHALL pass the plain-English check: sentences of at most 20 words,
   common words only.

## Requirement 7: Treasures

1. Winning Level 2 SHALL award **The Raven Codex**.
2. Winning Level 3 SHALL award **The Heart of the House**.
3. Each SHALL appear on the treasure card. Their images are AI-generated by the
   project owner, cut out like the witch images, and credited.
